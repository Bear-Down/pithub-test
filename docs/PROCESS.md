# PitHub Development Process

## 1. Purpose

PitHub uses Lewis University Software Engineering Course' **Scrum-based Agile development process** to organize work, deliver incremental improvements, and maintain effective team collaboration.

Our process focuses on:
- Incremental feature development.
- Continuous testing and improvement.
- Clear communication and accountability.

---

## 2. Sprint Structure

Our team works in **two-week sprints**.

Each sprint:
- Begins on Monday and ends on Sunday.
- Starts with Sprint Planning on the first Tuesday at 2:00 PM.
- Uses Discord for asynchronous daily updates.
- Ends with a Sprint Review and Sprint Retrospective.

Our team uses each sprint to plan, develop, test, review, and improve the PitHub application.
