Team Structure

Our team follows Scrum while maintaining a collaborative, self-organizing structure. All team members contribute to the development of the product. The Scrum Master and Product Owner also perform development responsibilities in addition to their Scrum-specific responsibilities.

Developers

All Developers are responsible for contributing to the design, development, testing, documentation, and delivery of the product. Developers collaborate with the Product Owner and Scrum Master to determine how work will be completed and help maintain a usable product increment.

Communication

The team uses Discord as its primary communication platform and GitHub as its primary platform for source control, project documentation, issues, and code collaboration.

Team members use Discord to:

* Provide updates on current work.
* Communicate progress and blockers.
* Ask questions and request assistance.
* Coordinate meetings and other team activities.
* Discuss project-related decisions.

Team members are expected to keep the rest of the team informed about their progress through Discord.

Team Collaboration

The team will work collaboratively and support one another throughout each sprint. Team members are responsible for communicating when they encounter blockers or anticipate difficulty completing assigned work.

The team will:

* Communicate regularly through Discord.
* Use GitHub to document and manage project work.
* Review and discuss important technical decisions as a group.
* Ask for assistance when blocked.
* Respect the contributions and opinions of other team members.
* Take responsibility for assigned work.
* Work toward completing a usable product increment each sprint.

Decision Making

The team will attempt to reach consensus when making significant project, technical, or process decisions. Team members are encouraged to provide input and explain their reasoning.

The Product Owner has responsibility for product priorities and backlog ordering, while technical implementation decisions are made collaboratively by the Developers.

Definition of Done

A Product Backlog item is considered Done when the agreed-upon acceptance criteria have been satisfied and the work meets the team’s quality expectations.

At minimum, the Definition of Done requires:

* The work described by the story has been completed.
* The acceptance criteria for the story have been satisfied.
* The implementation has been tested as appropriate.
* Necessary documentation has been updated.
* The completed work has been integrated into the product.
* At least one GitHub commit to the product branch that with a commit message Story [[STORY-NUMBER]] done.

The team may establish additional acceptance criteria for individual stories when necessary.
